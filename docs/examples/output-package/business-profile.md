# Acme Design

A 12-person design agency in India, billing in rupees. They work with three retainer clients
billed monthly and take on project work alongside it.

They are registered for GST, so every invoice needs a per-line GST breakdown and their GSTIN.
Customers pay by bank transfer and UPI, so invoices carry a UPI QR code.

They already use Google Workspace for email, WhatsApp Business for customer conversation, and
Tally through their accountant. Anything this system produces for the accountant should suit
Tally.

The owner will run Claude Code themselves. They are not technical. Explain things in plain
language and do not ask them to make technology choices.
